Privacy Policy

Count The Cost Scheduler

Last updated: July 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Count The Cost Scheduler ("the App," "we," "us") handles information. The App is a private, self-hosted tool operated by Count The Cost Bookkeeping to schedule and publish our own marketing content to our own connected social media accounts, including our own TikTok account. The App is not a consumer product and is not offered to third parties.

Who this applies to

The App is used solely by Count The Cost Bookkeeping to post content to accounts that we ourselves own and control. We do not create accounts for, collect data from, or provide services to members of the public through this App.

Information we access

When authorized by the account owner, the App uses official platform APIs (such as the TikTok Content Posting API) to:

  • Read basic profile information of the connected account (for example, the account's open ID and display name) to confirm the correct account is connected.
  • Upload and publish video content that we have created, to that same connected account.

The App does not access private messages, followers' personal data, or any information belonging to other users. It does not collect analytics on, track, or build profiles of any individuals.

How we use information

Access is used only to publish our own scheduled content and to confirm the correct destination account. We do not sell, rent, or share any information with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

How information is stored

Authorization tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in our own private infrastructure (GitHub Actions encrypted secrets) and are used only to call the platform APIs on our behalf. Video files and captions we schedule are stored in our own repository. We retain these only as long as needed to operate the scheduler.

Data sharing

We do not share data with any third party. Content is transmitted only to the platform (e.g., TikTok) to which we are publishing, through that platform's official API.

Revoking access

The connected account owner can revoke the App's access at any time through the platform's own settings (for example, TikTok Settings → Security & permissions → Manage app permissions). Revoking access immediately stops the App from posting.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to countingthecostbk@gmail.com.
